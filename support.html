Frequently asked

How do I add the Lock Screen widget?

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the widget area, and add Loro. You can also add the Home Screen widget by touching and holding the Home Screen.

How do I change the language I'm learning?

Open Loro → Settings → Learning Language. Free accounts choose one language; Loro Premium unlocks switching between all languages.

What does Loro Premium include?

Premium unlocks all languages, daily phrases, every widget, native audio, and unlimited reviews. It's available as a Monthly or Annual subscription (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase. You can start free and use the core widget forever.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Loro → Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your device: Settings app → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Loro. You can cancel anytime; during the free trial you won't be charged if you cancel before it ends.

How do I redeem a promo code?

Open Loro → Settings → Redeem Code and enter your code.

Is my data private?

Yes. Loro collects nothing and keeps everything on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
